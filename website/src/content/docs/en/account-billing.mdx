# Account & billing

Everything about your subscription, your team, and your data lives here. Billing is deliberately
simple: one flat monthly price per location, no per-order fees.

<Callout tone="note" title="Coming in a later release">
  The account dashboard and self-serve billing are part of an upcoming release. This page describes how
  it will work; today, reach out to support for any account changes.
</Callout>

## Your plan

Choose between [Starter, Growth, and Pro](/pricing). You can change plans anytime — upgrades take
effect immediately and we prorate the difference, downgrades apply at your next billing date. Your
menu, floor plan, and history are never affected by a plan change.

## Billing and invoices

<Steps>
  <Step title="Payment method">
    Add or update your card. We’ll email a receipt for every charge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invoices">
    Download past invoices anytime as PDF for your records.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Annual billing">
    Switch to annual billing to save the equivalent of two months.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Free trial

Every plan starts with a **14-day free trial** — no card required. At the end of the trial, add a
payment method to keep serving; nothing is charged automatically until you do.

## Team & access

Invite teammates and assign [roles](/docs/setup-restaurant) to control what each person can see and
do. Remove access instantly when someone leaves.

## Your data is portable

Your data is always yours. Export your menu, orders, and reports as CSV at any time, and Pro plans add
full [API access](/docs/reports-dashboard#api-access). If you ever cancel, you keep your exports.

<Note>
  Questions about billing? Every plan includes support, and Pro adds a dedicated success manager.
</Note>
