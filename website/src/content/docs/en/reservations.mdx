# Reservations

Reservations and walk-ins live in the same view as your floor plan, so the host always knows what’s
booked, who’s waiting, and what’s open right now.

## Taking a reservation

Add a booking with the party size, time, and guest details. Restaurant OS checks availability against
your [service hours](/docs/setup-restaurant) and current bookings, and suggests tables that fit.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Capture the details">
    Name, party size, date and time, plus any notes (allergies, occasion, seating preference).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign a table">
    Pick a suggested table or place the party manually on the floor plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    The booking appears on the timeline and the table is reserved for that slot.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The waitlist

For walk-ins when you’re full, add guests to the waitlist with a quoted time. As tables free up, the
host sees who’s next.

<Callout tone="tip" title="Quote times you can keep">
  The waitlist uses your live [turn-time data](/docs/tables-floor-plan) to suggest realistic quotes,
  so you’re not guessing how long a table will take.
</Callout>

## Seating a booking

When guests arrive, mark the reservation **seated** and it converts into an active table — ready for
the server to start [taking the order](/docs/pos-order-taking). No double entry.

## No-shows and notes

Track no-shows and keep guest notes that carry across visits, so regulars get the table they love and
the kitchen knows about allergies before the order is rung in.

<Note>
  Reservations & waitlist are included on the Growth and Pro plans. See [Pricing](/pricing).
</Note>
