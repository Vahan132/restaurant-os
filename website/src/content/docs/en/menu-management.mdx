# Menu management

Your menu is the single source of truth across the whole platform. Build it once and it powers the
POS, the kitchen, your QR menu, and your reports — all at the same time.

## How the menu is structured

A menu is organized into three layers:

- **Categories** group items (e.g. *Antipasti*, *Pasta*, *Cocktails*).
- **Items** are the dishes and drinks themselves, each with a name, price, and description.
- **Modifiers** capture choices and add-ons (e.g. *extra cheese*, *no onions*, *medium-rare*).

## Add your first items

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a category">
    Give it a name and an order position. Categories appear in this order on the POS and QR menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add items">
    Set a name, price, and an optional description and photo. Photos show on the QR menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach modifiers">
    Build reusable modifier groups (like *Temperature* or *Add-ons*) and attach them to any item.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout tone="tip" title="Reuse modifier groups">
  Define a modifier group once and attach it to many items. Editing the group updates every item that
  uses it — no hunting through the menu.
</Callout>

## 86’ing and availability

When you run out of something, mark it **86’d**. It instantly greys out on the POS and disappears
from the QR menu, so guests and staff never order what you can’t make. Bring it back with one tap.

## Pricing and tax

Prices are stored per item and combined with the tax rules from your
[restaurant setup](/docs/setup-restaurant). You can schedule price changes — useful for happy hour or
seasonal menus.

<Note>
  Multi-location operators on the Pro plan can keep a shared base menu and override prices or
  availability per venue. See [Reports & dashboard](/docs/reports-dashboard) to compare performance
  across locations.
</Note>
