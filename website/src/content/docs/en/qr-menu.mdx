# QR menu & customer ordering

Give guests a beautiful menu on their own phone — no app to download. They scan, browse, order, and
pay, and the order lands straight on the kitchen line.

## Publish your menu

Your QR menu is generated from the same [menu](/docs/menu-management) you already maintain. Publish it
and you’ll get a QR code and a link at your `your-place.restaurantos.app` address.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose what to show">
    Pick which categories and items appear on the public menu. Hide back-of-house or staff-only items.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate codes">
    Create one code for the whole venue, or per-table codes so orders attach to the right table
    automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Print and place">
    Download print-ready codes for table tents, stickers, or your window.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How ordering works

When a guest scans a per-table code, their order is tied to that table. They add items, choose
modifiers, and send — the ticket appears on the [Kitchen Display](/docs/kitchen-display) just like a
server-entered order.

<Callout tone="note" title="View-only or full ordering">
  You decide. Use the QR menu purely as a digital menu, or enable ordering and payment for full
  self-service. You can change this anytime.
</Callout>

## Live specials and 86’ing

Because the QR menu reads your live menu, anything you [86](/docs/menu-management) instantly disappears
for guests, and specials show up the moment you add them. No reprinting.

## Payment

Enable pay-at-table and guests can settle up from their phone. Payments flow into the same
[reports](/docs/reports-dashboard) as every other tender.
