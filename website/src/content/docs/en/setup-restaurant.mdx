# Set up your restaurant & subdomain

Your restaurant’s profile is the foundation everything else builds on — your public web address, your branding, your hours, and who on your team can do what.

## Claim your subdomain

Every restaurant gets a dedicated address. Choose a short, memorable name and you’ll be reachable at:

```text
https://your-place.restaurantos.app
```

This is where your QR menu lives and where staff sign in.

<Callout tone="warning" title="Choose carefully">
  Your subdomain appears on QR codes and links you’ll print and share, so pick something you’ll be
  happy with. Changing it later means reprinting any physical codes.
</Callout>

## Set the basics

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name & branding">
    Add your restaurant name, upload a logo, and pick an accent color. These appear on your QR menu
    and receipts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Locale & currency">
    Set your timezone, currency, and tax rules. Reports and receipts use these automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Service hours">
    Define when you’re open. Hours drive reservation availability and the “open / closed” state on
    your public menu.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Invite your team

Add the people who’ll use Restaurant OS and assign each a role. Roles control what someone can see
and do — a server doesn’t need the same access as an owner.

| Role | Typical access |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Everything, including billing |
| Manager | Menu, floor, reports, staff |
| Server | POS, tables, reservations |
| Kitchen | Kitchen Display only |

<Note>
  You can fine-tune permissions per role on Growth and Pro plans. See
  [Account & billing](/docs/account-billing) for what’s included.
</Note>
