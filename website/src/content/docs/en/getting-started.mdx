# Getting started

Welcome to Restaurant OS. This guide gives you the lay of the land and walks you from an empty account to your first real order. Most teams are taking orders the same afternoon they sign up.

<Callout tone="tip" title="The short version">
  Create your account → claim your subdomain → add a handful of menu items → open the POS and send a
  ticket. You can refine everything else once service is calm.
</Callout>

## What Restaurant OS is

Restaurant OS is a single platform that connects the parts of service that usually live in separate apps:

- **POS** for taking orders on the floor or at the counter
- **Kitchen Display (KDS)** for routing those orders to the line
- **Tables & reservations** for managing the room
- **QR menu & ordering** for letting guests order themselves
- **Reports** for understanding the night after it ends

Because they share one menu and one source of truth, a change in one place — 86’ing a dish, editing a price — shows up everywhere instantly.

## Before you begin

You don’t need to buy hardware. Restaurant OS runs in any modern browser, so a tablet, laptop, or existing POS terminal will do. For the kitchen, any screen with a browser works as a display.

## Your first afternoon

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up with your email. You’ll start on a 14-day free trial — no card required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim your subdomain">
    Pick a name like `your-place` and you’ll be live at `your-place.restaurantos.app`. See
    [Set up your restaurant & subdomain](/docs/setup-restaurant).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a few menu items">
    You don’t need the whole menu yet — add a category and a handful of dishes so you can test a real
    order. Details in [Menu management](/docs/menu-management).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the POS and send a ticket">
    Ring in an order and send it to the kitchen. Watch it appear on the
    [Kitchen Display](/docs/kitchen-display).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Where to go next

Once you’ve sent your first ticket, build out the rest at your own pace: design your
[floor plan](/docs/tables-floor-plan), publish your [QR menu](/docs/qr-menu), and turn on
[reservations](/docs/reservations).
